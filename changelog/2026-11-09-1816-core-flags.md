### 2026.11.09.1816

> An app defines each flag once and reads it with the user's toggle, overrides and an expiry day.

- Feat: `Flag` defines a flag once, with its name, default, title, expiry day and any older settings key.
- Feat: `FlagStore` resolves launch argument, environment, toggle, then default, and stores only non-default toggles.
- Feat: `FlagStore.isolated()` gives each test fresh toggles, and `changes()` streams each toggle change.
- Feat: `FlagCatalog.expired(asOf:)` lists flags past their expiry day, or with a day that does not exist.
- Docs: requirements 0001 to 0003, the architecture views, and the README.
