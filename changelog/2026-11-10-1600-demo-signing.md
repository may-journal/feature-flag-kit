### 2026.11.10.1600

> The demo app signs with the owner's team, so it installs on a real iPhone.

- Chore: the FlagDebugDemo project sets its bundle ID prefix and development team instead of turning signing off.
- Build: `make demo` builds the demo with no signing credentials, passing `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO` on the command line.
- Build: `make demo-install DEVICE=<udid>` signs the demo, installs it on a paired device and launches it.
- Docs: the README says how a fork signs the demo with its own team.
